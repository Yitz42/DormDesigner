How it works

A partnership in five steps

  1. Spring

    Agreement

    We agree on a pilot hall, delivery windows, rules for the rooms and the revenue share.

  2. Summer

    Orders and walk-throughs

    Families order through us. We walk each room and report paint, debris or damage to housing.

  3. Before move-in

    Setup

    We set up every ordered room in the agreed window, before students and families arrive.

  4. Each month

    Reporting

    We share satisfaction, maintenance and occupancy numbers for the pilot hall.

  5. May

    Pickup and review

    We collect everything after finals and review the semester with your team.

Start with a one-semester pilot

One hall, one semester. Each month we'd report on:

  1. Student satisfaction with their rooms
  2. Maintenance issues found and fixed before move-in
  3. Residents who return to campus housing

Questions

What housing teams ask us

What does a partnership cost the university?

Nothing. Students and families pay for packages, and a share of every package goes back to the housing program.

Does it change the furniture the university provides?

No. University furniture stays in the room. We add bedding, lighting, storage, decor and study equipment around it.

Who handles student and parent questions?

We do, before move-in and through the year, so they don’t add to housing staff workload.

How do you handle accessibility?

Every plan keeps clear paths to the bed, desk and closet. For students with accessibility needs, we lay the room out with them before move-in.

How do you keep rooms within residence hall rules?

We agree the rules with housing first: approved electrics, no open flames, removable wall hooks only, and nothing that blocks exits or sprinklers.
