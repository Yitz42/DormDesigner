How it works

From floor plan to finished room

Pick your hall

Choose your UMBC building and room type. We already have the floor plan, so we know where the bed, desk and wardrobe go.

Choose a package

Essentials, Basic or Deluxe. Each one is priced per student for the full school year.

Make it yours

Pick your colors, posters and lighting, and add a setup for your major, like an easel for art students.

Move in

We deliver and assemble everything before your move-in appointment. In May we pick it up, or you buy the pieces you want to keep.
