About us

We plan dorm rooms around the people who live in them

DormDesigner started as a student business in ENTR 200 at UMBC. We set out to take the stress out of furnishing a dorm room, and kept listening as we learned.

Our story

Built on what students told us

What we learned from students

We surveyed UMBC residents about their rooms. Most were content with the basics and enjoyed personalizing their space themselves, with posters, lamps and lights. Few said they'd pay for a design service on their own.

What they did complain about was rooms that weren't ready: paint and construction debris at move-in, maintenance, and layouts that didn't work for accessibility.

What we changed

So we work with the people who carry the cost and the responsibility: parents, who want their student settled and supported, and universities, who want rooms ready on time and students who do well.

Students still make the room theirs. They choose the colors, the posters and a setup for their major, and we handle everything else.

What we value

ComfortQualityAffordabilitySpeedStyle

College should feel a little easier every day. A comfortable, well-planned room helps students sleep better, study better and settle in.

Parents get one dependable service, universities get rooms that are ready on time, and students still make the space their own.
