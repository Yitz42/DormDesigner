Plan with Deluxe

Order with your roommates and pay less

Some pieces only need one per room. When everyone in a bedroom orders, the room gets one area rug and one mini-fridge, and each bathroom gets one bathroom set. The cost is split between you, so it comes off each person's price.

Bathroom set
$60 a year, split by everyone using the bathroom
Area rug
$90 a year, split by everyone in the bedroom
Mini-fridge
$150 a year, split by everyone in the bedroom

Two Deluxe roommates in a four-person suite each save $165 a year: $45 on the rug, $75 on the fridge and $45 on the bathroom set.

Monthly figures spread the yearly price over a 9-month school year. Add-ons for your major are extra.
