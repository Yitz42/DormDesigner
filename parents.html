Questions

What parents ask us

Who places the order, me or my student?

Either of you. There is one order per student, and we confirm the design with whoever placed it.

How do we pay?

Pay the yearly price at once, or monthly over the nine-month school year. Major setups are $75 on top.

Do roommates have to order too?

No. Each student can order on their own. When everyone in a bedroom orders, they share one rug and one mini-fridge and each pays less.

Can my student change the design?

Yes. Colors, posters and the major setup can change until two weeks before move-in.

What if something is damaged?

Normal wear is covered, and we repair or replace anything that breaks. Damage beyond normal use is billed at repair cost.

Is everything allowed in the residence hall?

Yes. We follow residence hall rules: no candles or open heating coils, and only removable hooks on the walls. Furniture UMBC provides stays in the room.

What happens at the end of the year?

We pick everything up after finals. Items marked Keep are yours, and anything else can be bought at a reduced price.
